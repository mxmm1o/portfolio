My Stack logo sources
=====================

All Iconify SVGs were fetched once from https://api.iconify.design/<set>/<name>.svg
and saved locally. There are no runtime external requests.

- Figma:              Iconify simple-icons:figma
- Photoshop:          Iconify devicon-plain:photoshop   (official Ps app tile, monochrome)
- Illustrator:        Iconify devicon-plain:illustrator (official Ai app tile, monochrome)
- Blender:            Iconify simple-icons:blender
- Spline:             Official mark at https://spline.design/images/spline_logo.png,
                      redrawn as a monochrome orb. No public SVG; not in Iconify or simple-icons.
- Canva:              Iconify bxl:canva                 (C in a circle)
- Google Workspace:   official 4-segment Google G, each block a different grey tone (fill-opacity)
- Slack:              Iconify mdi:slack
- Notion:             Iconify simple-icons:notion
- Claude:             Iconify simple-icons:claude       (starburst mark)
- GitHub:             Iconify simple-icons:github
- After Effects:      Iconify streamline-logos:adobe-after-effects-logo-block (CC BY 4.0, Streamline)
